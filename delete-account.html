HAYPO Delete Account

This page explains how HAYPO users can request deletion of their account and associated personal data.

How to Delete Your HAYPO Account

  1. Open the HAYPO application.
  2. Sign in to your HAYPO account.
  3. Open your Profile or Account Settings.
  4. Select Delete Account.
  5. Follow the instructions shown in the application to confirm the deletion.

What Happens When You Delete Your Account?

When your account deletion request is completed, HAYPO will delete personal information associated with your account, subject to any information that may need to be retained where required by law, security, fraud prevention, or legal obligations.

Data That May Be Deleted

  • Account information
  • Name and profile information
  • Email address and phone number
  • User profile data
  • Other personal information associated with your account

Need Help?

If you cannot delete your account through the HAYPO application, please contact HAYPO support for assistance with your account deletion request.
